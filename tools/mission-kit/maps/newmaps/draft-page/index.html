New maps: draft

Three new skirmish maps in the calm map style. They are not in the game. Each map has a test skirmish link, and all three links are also at the bottom.

Highlands

Play test skirmish

A river valley in the northern Vietnam style. Rice terraces climb from the river, bamboo covers the slopes, and pines and limestone karst towers stand on the high ground.

  • River with three stony fords; everything else is crossed on foot
  • Rice terraces stay clear of trees, ready for farms
  • Two hill ridges with passes, and 22 groups of karst towers that block movement
  • Bamboo and pine forests for wood, gold in the hills
  • Animals: deer, wild boar, kancil, seladang; wild bananas and berries

Deep Jungle

Play test skirmish

Thick landlocked rainforest. No sea and no boats. A stream with muddy fords and three pools winds through, and three trails are the only open ground between the bases.

  • About 2,800 trees: jungle giants, broadleaf, angsana, mango, bamboo thickets
  • Three trails link the bases; armies must use them or cut through
  • Stream and pools have fish; three muddy fords cross it
  • Durian trees (lots of food) and wild banana clumps (quick food)
  • Animals: deer, kancil, wild boar, seladang wild cattle

Mangrove Delta

Play test skirmish

A muddy mangrove coast. Both bases are on dry land in the north. South of them a belt of swamp water and mud flats is full of mangroves and nipa, cut by tidal creeks, with Orang Laut villages before the sea.

  • Dry north for both bases, linked by land
  • Swamp belt (water) crossed by five mud causeways
  • Four tidal creeks for boats, fish all along the shore
  • Three Orang Laut villages where the swamp meets the sea
  • Animals: deer, wild boar, kancil; wild bananas and berries

New animals and food

Animals run when hit, like deer, and leave a carcass to gather. Bigger animals take longer to kill and give more meat.
