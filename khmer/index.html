Ayutthaya's army is at the walls of Angkor. The city cannot be saved, but its people can. Hold the face gates against the war elephants while the monks, the treasury, the bronze statues and the sacred texts go south by river. Then get King Ponhea Yat and the royal family out. About 13–16 minutes.

How to play

  • Scroll: drag the map. Zoom: pinch (or the +/- buttons, or mouse wheel). Select: tap a unit or building; hold, then drag a box for a group. Double-tap a unit to pick all of its kind on screen. Tap it again to deselect.
  • Command: with units selected, tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack. Mouse: right-click.
  • The evacuation: select the royal compound and tap a load (monks, treasury, statues, texts) to send it. It walks to the South Gate, slips out by the postern door and waits at the River landing. Each load needs its own river barge (built at the landing), which rows it east to the end of the lake. The statues and texts are ready in the river phase; the royal family goes last.
  • The gates: fire arrows barely scorch your stone. Only Ayutthaya's war elephants break a face gate. Kill them with spearmen, Cart Ballistae and Ballista Elephants, and send rice farmers to repair a battered gate (faster with Ponhea Yat nearby). Select a gate to open it for a sally, but Ayutthaya can come in too.
  • The river: after six minutes Ayutthaya's war boats hold the far reach of the lake. Clear them with war canoes, and with ballistae on the shore.
  • The fall: when a gate breaks, or the siege drags on too long, the king of Ayutthaya attacks in person. Hold the royal compound until the court is ready, then send Ponhea Yat and the royal family to the royal barge.
  • Baray: a reservoir that boosts worked rice fields near it and speeds repairs to walls and towers beside it.
  • Win when the royal barge reaches the end of the lake; stars for each other load saved. Lose if the royal compound falls first, the royal family is taken, or Ponhea Yat falls in the last phase. Keys: A attack-move, S stop, F formation, . idle villager, H home, Space pause.
