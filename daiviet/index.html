How to play

  • Scroll: drag the map. Zoom: pinch (or the +/- buttons, or mouse wheel). Select: tap a unit or building; hold, then drag a box for a group. Double-tap a unit to pick all of its kind on screen. Tap it again to deselect.
  • Command: with units selected, tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack. Mouse: right-click.
  • Waves: ten Ming columns come down the three passes in the north. The bar under the resources shows what is coming and when. Between waves you can call the next one early for bonus gold.
  • Đình: the village hall takes every resource, and farms near it gather 25% faster. Select it and tap Drum to arm every villager nearby as Dân Binh militia (15 wood, 10 gold each), or select villagers and tap Arm. After 30 s militia can go back to the fields.
  • Forest ambush: soldiers standing still in a forest are hidden from the Ming until they strike, and their first blow from cover hits 50% harder. Hidden units fade and show a leaf.
  • Army: Thương Thủ spears beat cavalry and the general. Đao Thủ sabers break rattan shields and crossbows. Hỏa Đồng handgunners hit harder after standing still. The Citadel casts up to 3 Đại Bác cannon. Thầy Thuốc heal.
  • Heroes: Lê Lợi gives nearby troops +2 attack. Nguyễn Trãi talks beaten Ming soldiers into surrender. A fallen hero returns after 60 s.
  • Walls: build a bamboo hedge (Lũy tre) by tapping ✓ at each corner; it joins in straight runs. Gates open and close from their panel. Villagers repair damage.
  • Lose if the Citadel falls. Win by killing Liễu Thăng in wave 10. Keys: A attack-move, S stop, F formation, . idle villager, H home, Space pause.
