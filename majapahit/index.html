The Treasurer's GateThe fall of Singapura, from the sea

Command the Majapahit fleet against Singapura. Your flagship, the Jong Raya, is your base: launch boats of troops, win a foothold on Batam island, send a messenger to the king's treasurer, and raise the red and white banner over Bukit Larangan before Ayutthaya takes the city first. About 15–20 minutes.

How to play

  • Scroll: drag the map. Zoom: pinch (or the +/- buttons, or mouse wheel). Select: tap a unit or building; hold, then drag a box for a group. Double-tap a unit to pick all of its kind on screen. Tap it again to deselect.
  • Command: with units selected, tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack. Mouse: right-click.
  • The Jong Raya is your base. Select it (the Jong button, or H) to launch a boat: Kawula workers, Golok swords, Galah spears, Panah archers, fire ships, and later the messenger and Kelulus galleys. Each kind has a cooldown. She brings in a little food and wood, mends herself when no enemy is near, and patches boats beside her. If she sinks, the battle is lost; below 30% she cannot launch until mended above 60%.
  • Boats: select a laden boat (it shows 3/4) and tap the shore: it sails to the nearest beach and puts its people ashore. Tap troops onto one of your boats to climb aboard.
  • Batam is the only place the fleet can build. Land Kawula there and build a Lumbung (supply hut) to drop food, wood and gold. Omah houses add room for 5 more.
  • The Orang Laut village on Batam is neutral. Give gifts, or drive off the Siamese raid on it, and they join you (Kelulus galleys, +20% wood). Strike them and they fight for Singapura.
  • Plunder: burning the kampung, rice fields and the harbour outside the walls gives food and gold. Every foot soldier throws an obor (torch) when attacking a building, and archers shoot fire arrows. Timber burns fast and keeps burning; gates burn slowly and earth walls barely scorch.
  • The messenger walks in at the south gate dressed as a trader and must talk with the treasurer. Keep soldiers away from him. Or break a gate by force.
  • Win by holding Bukit Larangan, the palace hill, for 20 s with no defenders near. Lose if the Jong Raya sinks or the Ayutthaya bar fills. Keys: A attack-move, S stop, F formation, . idle worker, H flagship, Space pause.
