How to play

  • Scroll: drag the map. Zoom: pinch (or the +/- buttons, or mouse wheel).
  • Select: tap a unit or building. Group: hold, then drag a box. Double-tap a unit to pick all of its kind on screen. Deselect: tap the selected unit again, double-tap empty ground, or press Back.
  • Objectives: tap the gold bar under the resources to show or hide them.
  • Command: with units selected, tap the ground to move or an enemy to attack. Mouse: right-click.
  • Walls: select villagers and tap a damaged wall, gate or building to repair it. Select a gate to open or close it. New palisade costs wood.
  • Train: select the Barracks for soldiers, Bukit Larangan for villagers, the Port for Orang Laut boats.
  • Boats: select people, then tap an Orang Laut boat to climb aboard (6 per boat). Sail the boat to the marker on the map edge.
  • Heroes have a gold ring. If Parameswara falls, the mission is lost.
  • Keys: A attack-move, S stop, F formation, . idle villager, H home, Space pause.
