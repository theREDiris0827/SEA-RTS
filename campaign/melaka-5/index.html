How to play

  • Scroll: drag the map. Zoom: pinch (or the +/- buttons, or mouse wheel). Select: tap a unit or building; hold, then drag a box for a group. Double-tap a unit to pick all of its kind on screen.
  • Command: with units selected, tap the ground to move, an enemy to attack. Mouse: right-click.
  • The landings: the Portuguese row in from their carracks to two beaches: Upeh in the north, and the beach below the hill in the south. Red arrows show where the boats are heading. The stockades and the beach guns slow them down; meet them before they reach the bridge.
  • The bridge: if Portuguese stand on the bridge with no Melaka men near it, the red bar over it fills. If it fills, the bridge is lost. Any of your men on the bridge pushes it back.
  • Reinforce: train Bedil Diraja and Pahlawan at the Balai Laskar and the Istana, and send villagers to repair stockades and gates.
  • Lose if Raja Ahmad falls, the Istana falls, or the bridge is lost. Win when the Portuguese give up and fall back to their ships. Keys: A attack-move, S stop, Space pause.
