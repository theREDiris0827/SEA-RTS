The Fall of AngkorYasodharapura, 1431

Lead King Borommarachathirat II and the war elephants of Ayutthaya against the stone city of Angkor. Burn the timber town outside the moat, break a face gate with your Chang Suek, and raise the royal banner over the palace. About 15–20 minutes.

How to play

  • Scroll: drag the map. Zoom: pinch (or the +/- buttons, or mouse wheel). Select: tap a unit or building; hold, then drag a box for a group. Double-tap a unit to pick all of its kind on screen. Tap it again to deselect.
  • Command: with units selected, tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack. Mouse: right-click.
  • The siege: 1. burn the timber outer town and take the two face towers on the west causeway. 2. break a gate. 3. bring the king into the royal palace court and hold it for 20 s. The bar under the resources shows the next step; tap it for the full list.
  • Fire arrows: Phon Thanu archers set timber buildings alight. Laterite and sandstone only scorch. Each house you burn gives food and gold, and makes the Khmer sorties smaller.
  • Chang Suek: war elephants (3 population) from the Phaniat kraal. They are the only thing that breaks a gate, and an elephant that charges into a fight tramples the men around its target. Khmer spearmen and Ballista Elephants kill them: lead with Daab swordsmen and archers.
  • Households: a Ruean house next to a paddy that a village woman is working gives +15 fish every minute (up to 6). Build the same house on the water's edge and it becomes a raft house: send a woman to it and she fishes from its deck. Village women carry 25% more. Population cap 120.
  • The king rides a royal elephant and gives nearby troops +2 attack. If he falls he is carried to the camp and returns after 60 s.
  • Lose if the royal camp falls. Keys: A attack-move, S stop, F formation, . idle villager, H home, Space pause.
