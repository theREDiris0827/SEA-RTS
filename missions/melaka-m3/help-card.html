How to play

  • Scroll: drag the map. Zoom: pinch (or the +/- buttons, or mouse wheel). Select: tap a unit or building; hold, then drag a box for a group. Double-tap a unit to pick all of its kind on screen.
  • Command: with units selected, tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack. Mouse: right-click.
  • Wading: in this mission people can walk through shallow water, at half speed. Deep creeks still stop them.
  • Dusk: build a Port (it can stand on stilts in the shallows), train Penjajap, and win the Orang Laut village: select it and tap Gift, or drive off the Siamese scouts who raid it.
  • Torches: at night, select a villager and tap a mangrove with a gold ring. She wades out and ties a torch in its crown (40 wood, 10 seconds).
  • Courage: every burning torch wears down Siamese courage (the bar at the top). With fewer than 3 burning they take heart again. Landing parties come to put torches out: guard them. Sinking a ship costs them courage too.
  • Win when their courage breaks, or by sinking the whole fleet. Then chase the ships at dawn for a better score. Lose if Tun Perak falls or the camp's Balai falls. Keys: A attack-move, S stop, F formation, . idle villager, Space pause.
