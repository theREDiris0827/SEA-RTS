Melaka · Mission 3

Torches in the MangrovesBatu Pahat, c. 1456

A Siamese war fleet anchors off the mangrove coast and Tun Perak has too few ships to fight it. Get ready at dusk, then at night tie torches in the mangroves so the enemy sees a fleet that is not there. Keep them burning until the Siamese lose heart, then chase them at dawn. About 20 minutes.
