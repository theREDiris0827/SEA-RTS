How to play

  • Scroll: drag the map. Zoom: pinch (or the +/- buttons, or mouse wheel). Select: tap a unit or building; hold, then drag a box for a group. Double-tap a unit to pick all of its kind on screen. Tap it again to deselect.
  • Command: with units selected, tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack. Mouse: right-click.
  • Ages: select the Town Centre and tap the Age button. Each age costs food and gold and needs two of the buildings listed (the Golden Age needs the Istana and the Pangkalan). Ageing up opens new buildings and units, upgrades your archers to gunners and toughens your soldiers, but it also brings the next raid closer.
  • Raids: raiders come down the two roads in the east and land from the sea in the south. Arrows on the map edge show where, 25 s before. The bar under the resources says what is coming. Between raids you can call the next one early for bonus gold.
  • Fire: fire arrows set buildings alight. Villagers next to a burning building put it out and repair damage.
  • Army: Tombak spears beat elephants. Keris and Pahlawan break swordsmen. Pembedil and Bedil Diraja hit hard from range. Up to 3 Meriam cannon smash groups and ships. Tabib heal. Hang Tuah (Golden Age, Istana) gives nearby troops +2 attack and returns 60 s after he falls.
  • Walls: pick Palisade (or the stronger Wall from the Kingdom Age) and drag a line. A Gate set into the line lets your own people through and stops raiders. Villagers repair damage.
  • Lose if the Town Centre falls. Win by reaching the Golden Age, then killing the Siamese commander or breaking his army. Keys: A attack-move, S stop, F formation, . idle villager, H home, Space pause.
