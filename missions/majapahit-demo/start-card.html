The Treasurer's GateThe fall of Singapura, from the sea

Command the Majapahit fleet against Singapura. Your flagship, the Jong Raya, is your base: launch boats of troops, win a foothold on Batam island, send a messenger to the king's treasurer, and raise the red and white banner over Bukit Larangan before Ayutthaya takes the city first. About 15–20 minutes.
