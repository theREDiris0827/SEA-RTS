Rise of MajapahitEast Java, 1293–1336

Grow Raden Wijaya's clearing in the Tarik forest into the kingdom of the Palapa oath. Kediri, the Yuan fleet and rebel lords come for you while you build. Become the Kingdom of Majapahit, hold out against every raid, then break the rising of Sadeng and Keta. Kediri's war camp lies on the far side of the forest. About 30 minutes.
