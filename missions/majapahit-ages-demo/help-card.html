How to play

  • Scroll: drag the map. Zoom: pinch (or the +/- buttons, or mouse wheel). Select: tap a unit or building; hold, then drag a box for a group. Double-tap a unit to pick all of its kind on screen. Tap it again to deselect.
  • Command: with units selected, tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack. Mouse: right-click.
  • Ages: select the Town Centre and tap the Age button. This mission has two ages: the Village Age and the Kingdom Age. The Kingdom Age costs food and needs two of the buildings listed. The Bandar (trading post) can be built from the start on the landing beside a coast village; it brings in tribute gold. It opens new buildings, units and ships and toughens your soldiers, but it also brings the next raid closer.
  • Raids: raiders come down the two roads in the east and land from the river and the sea in the south. Arrows on the map edge show where, 25 s before. The bar under the resources says what is coming. Between raids you can call the next one early for bonus gold.
  • Fire: rebel fire arrows set buildings alight. Majapahit's red brick burns at half the usual rate. Villagers next to a burning building put it out and repair damage.
  • Army: Galah spears beat swordsmen. Golok swords break archers and crossbowmen. Bhayangkara (Kraton) are the royal guard. Panah archers shoot from behind the spears. Perahu Api fire ships burn enemy boats. Acaraki heal. Gajah Mada (Kingdom Age, Kraton) gives nearby troops +2 attack and returns 60 s after he falls.
  • Walls: pick Palisade (or the stronger Wall from the Kingdom Age) and drag a line. A Gate set into the line lets your own people through and stops raiders. Villagers repair damage.
  • Lose if the Town Centre falls. Win by reaching the Kingdom Age and breaking every raid, then killing the lord of Sadeng or breaking his army. Keys: A attack-move, S stop, F formation, . idle villager, H home, Space pause.
