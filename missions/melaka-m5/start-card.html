The Last Sultan of MelakaThe bridge over the Melaka river, 25 July 1511

Afonso de Albuquerque's carracks lie off the city. On St James' Day his men row for both beaches to seize the bridge that joins the Istana to the merchants' city. Hold the bridge with Raja Ahmad until the Portuguese fall back to their ships. About 8 minutes.
