Ayutthaya · Mission 1

The Island CityThe Chao Phraya, 1351

A sickness has emptied U Thong's town. Take his people down the river by barge to the island where three rivers meet, and found Ayutthaya there. Win over Lavo and Suphanburi by gifts, by marriage or by force, weather the rains, then hold the new palace while U Thong is crowned. About 20 minutes.
