How to play

  • Scroll: drag the map. Zoom: pinch (or the +/- buttons, or mouse wheel). Select: tap a unit or building; hold, then drag a box for a group. Double-tap a unit to pick all of its kind on screen.
  • Command: with units selected, tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack. Mouse: right-click.
  • Barges: select a barge and tap Load to take the nearest people aboard (or select people and tap the barge). Then tap a shore to land them. A barge carries 6.
  • Households: a house set down on the water floats as a raft house (on land it stays a house); tap it with a villager to send her fishing. A house beside a worked paddy field is a household too. Each household pays 15 food a minute.
  • Towns: select Lavo's or Suphanburi's town hall. Lavo wants 3 gift boats sent up the Lopburi river; Suphanburi a wedding procession across the fields. Or take a town by force: break its hall. Force is faster, but fewer troops join and your people work 10% slower.
  • The rains: for 3 minutes the flood plain goes under water. Paddy fields there stop and men wading are slowed. Raft houses keep fishing. Out of the rains, fields on the plain are richer.
  • Ages: select the Sala Klang to advance to the Kingdom Age. It opens the Wang (palace), the Wat, towers and walls, and puts your men in the royal purple.
  • Lose if U Thong falls, or the Sala Klang or the Wang falls. Win when U Thong has stood by the Wang for 90 seconds and is crowned. Keys: A attack-move, S stop, F formation, . idle villager, Space pause.
