How to play

  • Scroll: drag the map. Zoom: pinch (or the +/- buttons, or mouse wheel). Select: tap a unit or building; hold, then drag a box for a group. Double-tap a unit to pick all of its kind on screen.
  • Command: with units selected, tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack. Mouse: right-click.
  • The palace: select villagers, open the Economy tab and build the Preah Reachea Veang at the gold flag on the hill. Until it stands, villagers have nowhere to bring what they gather.
  • Floods: the ground tinted blue floods every few minutes (the clock at the top counts down). Flooded buildings lose health and flooded farms give nothing. Thnal (earth dikes, Economy tab) stop the water: drag a closed line between the river and your town. While the river rises, buildings that would flood glow red. The silt makes farms on the blue ground gather 20% faster.
  • The Baray: farms snap to the 8 dotted plots around it and gather 25% faster there. Villagers can drop food at its landing.
  • Villages: select the monks (saffron ring) and tap a river village with a gold flag. After their blessing, 3 farmers join you.
  • Win when the palace stands, the town came through a flood dry, 4 Baray plots are planted, 2 villages are won and all 3 raids are beaten. Lose if Ponhea Yat or the palace falls. Keys: A attack-move, S stop, F formation, . idle villager, Space pause.
