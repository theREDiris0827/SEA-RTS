Khmer · Mission 1

Where Four Rivers MeetChaktomuk, 1432–34

Angkor is lost. King Ponhea Yat brings the court down the river to Chaktomuk, where four rivers meet. Found the city on the hill, raise dikes before the flood, dig the first Baray, win over the river villages and hold off raiders from the west. About 20 minutes.
