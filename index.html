How to play

  • Scroll: drag the map. Zoom: pinch (or the +/- buttons, or mouse wheel).
  • Select: tap a unit or building. Group: hold, then drag a box. Double-tap a unit to pick all of its kind on screen.
  • Command: with units selected, tap the ground to move, a tree, berries or gold to gather, an enemy to attack. Mouse: right-click.
  • Build: select villagers, pick a building, drag it into place, then tap the green tick (or Place). The red cross cancels.
  • Train: select the Town Centre or Barracks and tap a unit. Double-tap the ground (or right-click) to set where new units gather; a single tap deselects.
  • Fishing: build a Trading Port on the shore, train boats, send them to fish. The Port also earns gold from trade.
  • Houses raise your population limit. Raids come on a timer, so keep a few warriors home.
  • Win by destroying the red Town Centre. Keys: A attack-move, S stop, F formation, . idle villager, H home, Space pause.
